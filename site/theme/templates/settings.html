Home Management

Download a backup before resetting your home.

1. Backup

Stops your current challenge and downloads your files as a .tar.gz archive. Your files stay in place.

2. Reset

Permanently deletes all home files, including files excluded from your backup.

Download a backup first to enable Reset.

Files larger than 10 MB are excluded from backups. Backups are limited to 1 GiB.

Keep your download; backups are not retained. Start a new challenge to continue.
